# Core Messaging & Positioning

The primary objective of all website copy is to position **Turn-Metal Engineering (Pty) Ltd.** as a premier, high-precision engineering and maintenance partner for South Africa's milling, grain, and agricultural processing industries.

### Core Value Proposition Statement
> "Precision machining, specialized mechanical equipment manufacturing, and dependable on-site maintenance tailored for the South African milling and grain industry."

---

# Voice & Tone Guidelines

| Attribute | How It Sounds | What to Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Authoritative & Technical** | Use precise engineering terminology (e.g., *tolerances, wear resistance, pitch accuracy, dynamic balance*). | Vague jargon or overly trendy marketing buzzwords. |
| **Direct & Solution-Focused** | Clear, concise statements highlighting reduced downtime and extended equipment lifespan. | Fluffy, overly wordy introductions. |
| **Reliable & Confident** | Reassuring, industrial precision messaging that emphasizes quality and accuracy. | Unverifiable claims, passive language, or terms like "heavy structural fabrication". |

---

# Terminology Standards

To accurately reflect Turn-Metal's capabilities without overstating shop scope, all site copy adheres to these strict terminology guidelines:

* **Approved Terms:** Precision machining, custom component manufacturing, mechanical assemblies, material handling equipment (screw conveyors & elevator drums), specialized on-site servicing.
* **Prohibited Terms:** Heavy equipment fabrication, heavy structural steelwork, mega-structures.

### 3. Services Page (`/services`)
Each service block is structured to answer three questions for plant engineers: *What is it?*, *What specs/capabilities do we offer?*, and *Why does it matter for your plant?*

* **Precision Machining:** CNC & conventional turning, shaft fabrication, gear re-machining, bushes, and high-tolerance custom components.
* **Screw Conveyor Manufacturing:** Standard and custom pitch flighting, trough fabrication, hardfacing, and shaft alignment.
* **Bucket Elevator Drum Manufacturing:** Precision-balanced head and tail drums, rubber lagging, and heavy-wall tube construction.
* **On-Site Plant Maintenance:** Emergency breakdown repairs, scheduled maintenance, and specialist roller changes for flour and maize mills.

### 4. Industries We Serve (`/industries`)

* **Feed Mills & Agricultural Plants:** Focus on high-wear components and custom mechanical assemblies.
