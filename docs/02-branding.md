# Brand Overview

The Turn-Metal visual identity reflects heavy engineering, precision machining, and industrial durability. The badge logo combines deep brushed blues with 3D chrome/silver metal typography, conveying strength, reliability, and modern South African manufacturing excellence.

# Typography System

The logo features a bold, 3D beveled serif with heavy weight and industrial presence. For web performance, we pair this look with web-safe, accessible **Google Fonts**.

### 1. Headings (Primary Display)

* **Recommended Font:** **[Cinzel](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel)** or **[Graduate](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Graduate)** *(Google Fonts)*
* **Style:** Bold / Extra Bold (Uppercase)
* **Application:** `H1`, `H2`, `H3` section titles, hero headlines.
* **Alternative (Slab Serif):** **[Alfa Slab One](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Alfa+Slab+One)** or **[Roboto Slab](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto+Slab)** for heavy industrial titles.

### 2. Body Text & Technical Data

* **Recommended Font:** **[Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)** or **[Roboto](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Roboto)** *(Google Fonts)*
* **Style:** Regular / Medium
* **Application:** Paragraphs, equipment specifications, service bullet points, contact forms.

# Brand Motifs & Icons

* **Core Symbol:** Industrial Metal Badge with Oversized "M" and Horizontal Accent Lines.
* **Design Motifs:** Thin horizontal silver divider lines running across sections to echo the logo’s framing lines.

### Website Icon Style Guide

All UI icons (services, capabilities, features) should follow a uniform style:

1. **Gears & Precision Tools:** Solid or thick-stroke icons representing precision machining, lathes, and milling plants.
2. **Material Handling Symbols:** Conveyor screws, bucket elevators, and industrial drums.
3. **Finish:** Metallic silver (`#CBD5E1`) on dark blue cards, or deep blue (`#1E3A8A`) on light cards.

---

# Brand Personality

Turn-Metal should consistently communicate:

* **Strong & Durable:** Built to handle heavy agricultural and milling industrial demands.
* **Precise & Engineered:** High-tolerance manufacturing and exact specifications.
* **Trustworthy & Local:** Reliable South African engineering expertise and responsive service.
* **Premium Quality:** Professional finish in both the workshop and online presence.

---

# Website Design Guidance

### Hero Section Background Idea

* A dark, high-contrast industrial background: a deep brushed steel or dark navy blue gradient (`#0F172A` to `#1E3A8A`) with a subtle overlay of a precision lathe in action or machining sparks, framed by thin silver horizontal accent lines.

### Button Styling (Tailwind / CSS)

* **Primary Call-to-Action ("Request a Quote"):** Industrial Gold background (`#D97706`), sharp or slightly rounded corners (`rounded-md`), bold white uppercase text, with a dark shadow for depth.
* **Secondary Action ("Our Services"):** Dark blue background with a thin brushed silver border (`border-2 border-slate-300`) and white text.
